- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 (v1.1.0) · **Status:** Planned

## What it does

Users propose a Personal persona, filter or template as Official. Admins approve it (it becomes Official and Admin-owned, and the proposer is kept on the Proposal) or reject it with an optional note. The proposer sees the outcome.
