# Emails to the sales rep about bookings (F22, F27)

The owner's wording (2026-11-03), used word for word by the app for bookings from the Lumor
booking page (F22) and from Calendly (F27). `{…}` are filled in by the app. Lines in
_italics_ explain how the app adapts a line; they are not part of the email.

- "demo" is the event's **name in emails** (set on the event; for example "Lumor demo").
- The answer rows list every question on the booking form, in order. An empty answer shows "Nothing added".

## 1. Booked

Subject: Demo booked: {Name}, {Company} · {date} at {time} IST

{Name} ({Role} at {Company}) booked a 20-minute demo.

_({Role} comes from a booking question about their role or title. Without one, the line reads
"{Name} ({Company}) booked a {duration}-minute demo.")_

When: {date}, {time} IST
Google Meet: {meeting link}
Research they run: {answer to question 3}
Wants to see: {answer to question 4, or “Nothing added”}

Sales Hub has already logged “Meeting booked”, moved them to Meeting scheduled, and paused their outreach plan.

_(When the person isn't on a plan, the line ends at "Meeting scheduled.")_

Before the call, review the conversation and pick your demo angle.

[ Open in Sales Hub ] → {Sales Hub link to the person’s page}

If no matching person is found in Sales Hub (for example, the link was shared by a colleague), swap the middle line for:

This person wasn’t in Sales Hub, so a new lead was created and assigned to you. Check the company and persona.

## 2. Cancelled

Subject: Demo cancelled: {Name}, {Company}

{Name} cancelled the demo on {date}. Reason: {cancel reason, or “None given”}. Their plan has resumed so you can follow up.

_(When the person isn't on a plan, the last sentence reads "Follow up when it suits.")_

Not in the owner's copy; the app uses the same style:

Subject: Demo moved: {Name}, {Company} · {new date} at {new time} IST

{Name} moved the demo from {old date}, {old time} IST to {new date}, {new time} IST.
