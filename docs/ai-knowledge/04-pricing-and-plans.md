## What the AI may say about pricing

- Quote plan prices and limits exactly as above, in USD. Add the INR figure only if the prospect is in India or asks.
- **Annual vs monthly:** give both prices. Annual is the "Save 30%" option on the pricing page; the simplest accurate phrasing is "monthly billing costs about 30% more than annual."
- **Panel recruitment** is ordered separately on paid plans: the team sends a quote per order (see `03`). Business includes $50 of panel credits every month. The Free plan and free trials don't include panel access.
- Recommend a plan by matching the prospect's studies and responses per month and team size to the table.
- Free is a good first step for anyone who wants to try Lumor without a sales call. To test with real panel respondents, they need a paid plan.
- Business suits teams that need several users, SSO and regular panel studies.
- Enterprise suits agencies and large teams with high volume or security reviews. It's an annual contract; offer a call.

## What the AI must not do

- **No discounts, custom prices, extra credits or free months.** Say "Let me check what we can do on pricing" and flag it for the account owner.
- **Don't quote a panel cost per response.** It depends on the audience; the team sends a quote within 24 hours of a request.
- **Don't say "unlimited teammates" or "no per-seat fees"** for Free, Starter or Business. The user limits above apply.
- **Don't describe Enterprise as "no lock-in" or monthly.** It's annual only.
- Don't mention the SMB QR feedback plan. It's **upcoming** and its price isn't set (see `07`).
- Don't discuss taxes, GST, invoicing or payment methods. Flag them for the team.
