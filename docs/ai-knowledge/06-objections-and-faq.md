# Objections and FAQ

Approved answers. Adapt the wording to the conversation, but don't add facts that aren't in these files. Keep replies to 2–4 lines and end with one next step.

## Objections

**"We already use [Qualtrics / SurveyMonkey / Typeform / Google Forms]."**
Acknowledge it. Ask what they use for recruitment and analysis. Most teams combine a survey tool with a separate panel and analysis step; Lumor does all three in one place. For agencies, offer Intercepts: keep the current tool and route respondents through Lumor.

**"We have our own panel."**
Good: Lumor works with your own users, and Intercepts adds logic, quotas and analysis on top of an existing panel without switching tools. Intercepts is available on every plan, including Free.

**"Not now / no budget right now."**
Respect it. Ask when research planning usually happens, and suggest the Free plan so they can try Lumor in the meantime. Don't push.

**"It's too expensive."**
Ask which tools and vendors they pay for today. One Lumor subscription replaces the survey tool, panel access and reporting. Never offer a discount; flag pricing questions for the account owner.

**"We don't trust AI with research."**
Lumor AI proposes changes; the researcher reviews, applies or undoes each one. It never edits a survey silently, and analysis answers are drawn from the actual responses.

**"Our surveys have complex logic."**
That's where the Logic Map helps: every branch and path on one canvas, with issues flagged while building. Offer to show it on one of their surveys.

**"Is the panel quality good?"**
Panelists are vetted through partner panels, with email authentication, double opt-in, fingerprinting, geo-IP checks, quality scoring and profiles refreshed every 6 months. The team checks feasibility and sends a quote before anything is paid.

**"We're a small team."**
The Free plan works for one person; Starter covers 3 users. Teammates can comment and collaborate on studies.
