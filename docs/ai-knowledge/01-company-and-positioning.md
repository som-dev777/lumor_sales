# Company and positioning

## Who Lumor is

- **Company:** Lumor Technologies Pvt. Ltd., based in Bengaluru, India.
- **Product:** Lumor (lumor.tech, app at app.lumor.tech), an AI-powered research platform.
- **What it does:** one platform to build a survey, recruit respondents, collect responses, analyse them with AI and share client-ready reports.
- **Who it's for:** researchers and research teams worldwide: research agencies, independent consultants, insights and brand teams, product managers, UX researchers, designers and marketers.

## The pitch

**One line:** Lumor takes a research question to a shareable answer in one place: AI builds the survey, a partner panel or your own audience responds, and AI writes the report.

**The problem it solves:** teams typically stitch together a survey tool, a panel vendor and a separate analysis or reporting tool, then pay for each. Lumor replaces that stack with one subscription.

**Three reasons teams switch:**
1. **One platform, end to end:** build, recruit, field, analyse and report without exporting between tools.
2. **AI that stays under your control:** Lumor AI proposes changes, you review and apply them, and you can undo. It never edits a survey silently.
3. **Complex surveys made visible:** the Logic Map shows every branch and respondent path on one canvas and flags issues as you build.

## Positioning rules

1. **Global first.** Present Lumor as a global research platform for teams anywhere. Never describe it as "India-first" or "for Indian companies". Being based in Bengaluru is a fact, not a positioning.
2. **Live and proven.** Describe Lumor as a working product teams use today. Never use language that sounds like early development, beta or problem discovery ("we're exploring", "we're validating", "would you use…").
3. **The panel is partner-sourced.** Respondents come from vetted partner panels. Never call the panel proprietary, "our own panel" or "owned".
4. **Only live features.** Describe only features listed in `02` and `03`. Upcoming features follow `07-upcoming-features.md`.
5. **No competitor attacks.** It's fine to say teams often use tools such as Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Google Forms, Alchemer or Maze alongside a separate panel and analysis tool. Never state a competitor's prices, limits or flaws as fact.

## Voice and tone

- Plain, friendly and specific. Short sentences.
- Sound like a knowledgeable colleague, not a salesperson. No hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-in-class").
- Lead with the prospect's situation, then one relevant capability. Never list every feature.
- LinkedIn messages: 2–4 lines. Cold openers: 2 lines.
- One clear next step per message, usually a 20-minute demo.
