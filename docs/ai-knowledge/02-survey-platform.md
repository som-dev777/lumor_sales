# The survey platform (all Live)

Everything in this file is available in Lumor today. Source: lumor.tech and lumor.tech/platform, verified 2026-10-22.

## 1. Building a survey

- **Ask Lumor AI:** describe the research goal in plain English and Lumor AI drafts a full survey: the questions, their order and the tone. You keep refining it by chatting.
- **AI as a copilot, not an autopilot:** Lumor AI works as propose → review → apply → undo. It never changes a survey without the user approving the change. This matters to methodical researchers who don't trust black-box AI.
- **100+ expert-built templates.**
- **14+ question types,** including NPS, ranking, best-worst (MaxDiff-style), image choice, star and scale ratings, slider, matrix and open text. Don't name question types beyond these.
- **Sections, settings, live preview and publishing.**
- **On-brand surveys:** save brands (logo and theme) and apply one to any survey with "Add branding". Paid plans can remove Lumor branding.

## 2. Logic and survey flow

- **Logic Map:** a visual canvas showing every branch, follow-up and respondent path in the survey.
- **Built-in issue checks** flag logic problems while you build, before any respondent sees them.
- **Skip logic, conditional paths, piping** (carrying earlier answers forward) and **follow-up questions**.
- Best shown in a demo with a complex, branching survey. It's the clearest differentiator for experienced researchers.

## 3. Quotas

- **Smart quotas** at three levels: the whole study, a single question, or a combination of answers (for example, age group × region).
- Fielding stops automatically when each quota cell fills.
- Supports representative samples.

## 4. Collecting responses

- **Multiple collectors per study:** web link, email, WhatsApp, QR code and website embed.
- **Live response tracking:** watch responses arrive while the study is in field.
- **Screeners and email confirmation** for respondents.
- **Internal and external panels managed in one place:** your own users and the Lumor Panel side by side (see `03`).
- Only completed responses count toward plan limits; partial or abandoned ones don't.

## 5. Analysis

- **AI Results:** automatic summaries, theme detection and highlights as soon as responses arrive.
- **Chat with your data:** ask questions in plain English and get answers drawn from the actual responses.
- **Per-question analysis, individual submissions and live dashboards.**

## 6. Reporting

- **AI Doc and Slides:** generate a stakeholder-ready report or slide deck in one click, including from a chat conversation.
- **AI reports are available on the Free plan** (limits per plan in `04`).

## 7. Collaboration

- **Real-time collaboration** with roles and permissions. The number of users depends on the plan (see `04`).
- **Comments on surveys:** general or on a specific question, with @mentions, replies and resolve.
- **Sharing** of studies and reports.
- **Organisation:** Organisation → Workspace → Project → Folder → Study, plus recents and starred items.

## 8. Integrations and data

- Team chat, docs and sheets connections, and real-time response alerts.
- Export to your data stack; API integrations on Business; full API on Enterprise.
- Integrations are set up per client based on their needs. For any specific named tool, say "Let me confirm that integration for you" and raise a flag. Don't promise a named integration.
- Export or delete your data at any time.

## 9. Security and admin

- SSO with Google or Microsoft (Business and above), Google sign-in, and invitations.
- Role-based access.
- Data encrypted in transit.
- Dedicated customer success manager and SLA on Enterprise.
- For certifications (ISO, SOC 2), data residency, DPAs or security questionnaires: never answer from memory. Say "I'll get our security details to you" and flag it.
