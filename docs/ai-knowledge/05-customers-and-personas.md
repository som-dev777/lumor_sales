# Customers and personas

Use this to tailor every message: match the persona, lead with their pain, and offer one relevant capability.

## Priority segments

| Priority | Segment | Why they buy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | Full-service research agencies (market research, CATI and panel agencies) | Project budgets; need panel access, complex logic and quotas, fast client reporting; Intercepts lets them keep their tools |
| Tier 1 | Independent research consultants | Need panel access and client-ready reports without buying several tools |
| Tier 1 | D2C and consumer brand insights teams | Frequent concept, ad, NPS and customer studies; need speed and panel access |
| Tier 2 | In-house research and insights teams at mid-size and large companies (fintech, apps, digital businesses) | Several tools today; want one platform and AI analysis |
| Tier 2 | Ad and creative agencies | Pre-campaign concept and ad testing with fast turnaround |
| Tier 2 | Product and design teams without a dedicated researcher | Want real answers from real users without hiring a researcher |

## Personas

### Research & Insights (Tier 1)
- **Titles:** Head of Research, Research Director, Research Manager, Consumer Insights Manager, Market Research Manager, UX Researcher, Research Ops.
- **Uses today:** Qualtrics, SurveyMonkey, Typeform, Google Forms, Dovetail.
- **Pains:** juggling a survey tool, a panel vendor and analysis; complex branching that's hard to check; reporting takes days.
- **Lead with:** one platform from survey to panel to report; the Logic Map; AI that proposes changes for review.

### Product & Design (Tier 2)
- **Titles:** Product Manager, Head of Product, Product Designer, UX Designer, Head of Design.
- **Uses today:** Maze, Ballpark, Typeform, Google Forms.
- **Pains:** no researcher on the team; decisions made on opinion; recruiting real users is slow.
- **Lead with:** run real research with recruited users in days, without hiring a researcher. Don't pitch prototype or usability testing; it's upcoming (`07`).

### CX, Brand & Marketing (Tier 2)
- **Titles:** CX Manager, Brand Manager, Product Marketing Manager, CMO, Strategic or Planning Director.
- **Uses today:** SurveyMonkey, Typeform, in-house NPS tools, agencies.
- **Pains:** NPS and concept tests outsourced or slow; open-ended answers never get read.
- **Lead with:** run NPS and concept or ad tests in-house, with AI summarising open-ended answers.

### Founders & Agency Directors (Tier 1)
- **Titles:** Founder, Co-founder, Managing Director, CEO, Research Director.
- **Uses today:** Qualtrics, Forsta/Decipher, Alchemer.
- **Pains:** margins squeezed by tool and panel costs; slow turnaround for clients.
- **Lead with:** for agencies, Intercepts (route your own panel through Lumor without switching tools); for startups, one tool for all research.

### Independent Researchers & Consultants (Tier 1)
- **Titles:** Research Consultant, Independent Researcher, Freelance UX Researcher.
- **Uses today:** Typeform, SurveyMonkey, Google Forms.
- **Pains:** sourcing respondents for each client; building reports by hand.
- **Lead with:** panel access plus client-ready reports in one subscription.

## Sales stages (for context in replies)

Prospect → Contacted → Connected → Replied → Meeting scheduled → Meeting done → Proposal sent → Trial → Paid → Active customer. Lost can happen at any stage.

- **Before a meeting:** the goal of every reply is a 20-minute demo on a study like theirs.
- **After a meeting:** the goal is sending a proposal or rates, then a trial or purchase.
- **After purchase:** onboarding and support, often via a WhatsApp group with the customer and their account manager.
