# Respondents: Lumor Panel and Intercepts (all Live)

Source: lumor.tech, lumor.tech/platform, the app's recruitment screen and Somnath's confirmations, verified 2026-10-22.

## Lumor Panel

- **What it is:** access to respondents through vetted partner panels, ordered and managed inside Lumor. **Always describe it as partner-sourced. Never call it proprietary or Lumor-owned, and never name the partner panels.**
- **Reach:** 3M+ vetted panelists across 30+ countries.
- **Targeting:** 50+ attributes, including country and age, plus custom screening questions.
- **Quality controls:** email authentication, double opt-in, digital fingerprinting, geo-IP checks, quality scoring, and panelist profiles refreshed every 6 months.
- **Bring your own users:** teams can field to their own customers or users alongside, or instead of, the panel.
- **Who can use it:** paid plans (Starter, Business, Enterprise), paid per order. Business includes $50 of panel credits every month. The Free plan and free trials have no panel access.

## How a panel order works (in the app)

1. In the survey's recruitment step, create a **target group**: name it, choose the number of responses (up to 5,000 per target group), the country, the age range (18–99) and any other criteria. Optionally add custom screening questions and schedule when recruitment starts.
2. Click **Request for proposal.** The Lumor team checks feasibility and sends a quote (price per response and total, in USD) **within 24 hours**.
3. Review the quote and **proceed to payment.**
4. Recruitment starts, and responses arrive in the study with live tracking.

A target group can't be edited after it's created; to change targeting, create a new one.

What not to say:
- Don't promise feasibility, incidence, sample size or a price per response for any audience. Say "We can check feasibility and cost for that audience. You'll have a quote within 24 hours."

## Intercepts (for agencies and teams with their own tools)

- **What it is:** a hosted routing layer. Teams keep running surveys in their existing tool (Typeform, SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics or a custom tool) and route respondents through Lumor.
- **What it adds:** a branded start page, identity preservation, metadata pass-through, completion callbacks, outcome mapping (complete, screen-out, over-quota) and intercept analytics.
- **Plans:** available on every plan, including Free. Each Intercepts project counts as one study toward the plan's monthly study limit.
- **Why it matters:** an agency gets value without switching tools. This is the strongest first conversation with research agencies and panel or CATI agencies.
